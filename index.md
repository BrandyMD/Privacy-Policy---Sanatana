  <h1>Privacy Policy for Sanātana</h1>
  <p class="date"><em>Last updated: October 23, 2026</em></p>

  <p>This Privacy Policy describes how <strong>Sanātana</strong> ("we", "our", or "the app") handles user information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to transparency and protecting your privacy.</p>

  <h2>1. Local Processing & Data Storage</h2>
  <p>Our core educational and reflective features operate locally on your device without sending sensitive personal content to external servers:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Saved Progress & Favorites:</strong> When you save a mantra, favorite a wisdom quote, or complete a lesson, that data is stored locally on your device. We do not transmit, upload, or store your personal progress or collections on any remote cloud servers.</li>
    <li><strong>Audio Pronunciation Guides:</strong> The app utilizes on-device Text-to-Speech (TTS) and web audio features to play mantras. It may occasionally route text through third-party TTS APIs (such as Google TTS) solely to stream pronunciation guides back to your device. The app does not access your microphone, nor does it record or transmit your voice.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>2. Third-Party Services, Analytics & Advertising</h2>
  <p>To support ongoing development and understand app usage, the app integrates third-party services provided by Google LLC. These services collect certain data automatically:</p>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Google AdMob:</strong> We use AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect and process device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID), approximate location data derived from IP addresses, and interaction metrics to serve relevant or contextual ads.</li>
    <li><strong>Google Analytics for Firebase:</strong> We utilize Firebase Analytics to understand user engagement, feature usage, and retention trends. Firebase automatically collects pseudonymous identifiers, device hardware models, operating system versions, and standard in-app event interactions.</li>
  </ul>
  <p>For more details on how Google processes and protects data collected through partner apps, please review <a href="https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites" target="_blank" rel="noopener">How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services</a> and the <a href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Google Privacy Policy</a>.</p>

  <h2>3. Data Retention and Control</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Ad Identifiers:</strong> You can reset or limit your device's Advertising ID at any time through your Android system settings (under <em>Settings &gt; Google &gt; Ads</em>).</li>
    <li><strong>App Data:</strong> You can remove all locally cached preferences, saved mantras, and lesson progress by clearing the app data in your device settings or by uninstalling the application. You can also export a copy of your progress directly within the app before clearing it.</li>
  </ul>

  <h2>4. Children’s Privacy</h2>
  <p>The app is intended for general audiences seeking educational, spiritual, and philosophical content. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.</p>
